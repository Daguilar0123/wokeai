# Using the wokeai name and marks

Decided by Danny (Daniel Aguilar), October 18, 2026. This is the community-use policy the README promised. It is short on purpose. If it does not answer your question, ask (see the end).

## What this covers

- **The name** *wokeai*: one lowercase word, even at the start of a sentence.
- **The spoken form**: *wōk•ē•ī*. Say it that way; do not rename it.
- **The marks** in `marks/`. The roots mark (`roots-official-*.svg`, `roots-blueprint*.svg`, `a-roots.svg`, the favicon cuts, the animated versions) is the wokeai mark. The Heard mark (`heard*.svg`, `b-heard.svg`) is reserved for the podcast *wahkeeye* and is not for community use; ask before using it anywhere.

The name, spoken form, and marks belong to Daniel Aguilar, all rights reserved. They are not covered by the code license (AGPL-3.0) or the research license (CC BY-SA 4.0). Using the code is not permission to speak as wokeai.

## Free for the community

You do not need to ask for any of these:

- **Meetups and talks.** Flyers, slides, and event pages for a wokeai meetup, reading group, workshop, or a talk about wokeai.
- **Forks and tools.** A fork's README or a tool page saying it is built on, or built for, wokeai, with a link back to this repo or wokeainow.com.
- **Personal sites and bios.** Saying "part of wokeai" or "member of wokeai" on your site, profile, or bio.
- **Writing about wokeai.** Articles, posts, zines, research, and criticism that name wokeai and show the mark to identify it.

Community members may fly the wokeai mark, unmodified, with a "member of wokeai" or "part of wokeai" line. Use the SVGs as given, keep the pronunciation line (*pronounced wōk•ē•ī*) under the wordmark where there is room, and follow the brand book's clear space, size, and color rules in [`design/system/README.md`](design/system/README.md).

## Ask first

Write before you do any of these:

- **Products and merch.** Selling anything that carries the name or mark: apps, services, shirts, stickers, mugs.
- **Implying endorsement.** Anything that makes it look like wokeai made, approved, or runs a project, event, or person's work. "Member of wokeai" is fine; "wokeai presents" is not, unless it does.
- **Altering the mark.** Redrawing, recoloring outside the tokens, or pairing it with anything else. The brand book's rules still hold: a version with an eye, rays, fist, chains, torch, lightbulb, face, or an X will not get a yes.

## Never

- **Government bodies, police, and surveillance or AI vendors** may not use the name or marks in any form. This answers Executive Order 14319; the brand is not available to the institutions it answers.

## Where to ask

Email **hello@wokeainow.com**. Say what you want to use, where, and for how long. Danny chose this address on October 18, 2026 as the public contact for the brand; it is the address in the site's email plan.
