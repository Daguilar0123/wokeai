wokeai (pronounced wōk•ē•ī). A grassroots, open-source community for people using their own AI to push back against systemic oppression. Open tools, shared in common. Starting small. wokeainow.com

**About:**

wokeai is one word, always lowercase, and it's said *wōk•ē•ī*. The spelling says one thing to a glance. We'll tell you how it's said.

We're a grassroots community for people who use their own AI to push back against systemic oppression: students, independent researchers, organizers, and builders. Not a product. A place to gather, a list of open tools that already exist, and the people who want to build the next ones.

Three things hold it up. Open source: tools held in common, with the code where everyone can read it. Liberation: starting from the people with the least power, not the most. Community: nobody does this alone. The roots are the point.

We're in our first days and there's no budget, so we start with what we have. Read the tool list, bring the ones you use, and tell someone.

**Overview** (2,000-character field; this is 1,977):

wokeai is a web app under construction. The community and the tool sharing are too. What’s here now is the idea, the brand, and the code.

As a brand, wokeai is about using AI to fundamentally challenge systems of oppression. They come in many forms: white supremacy, patriarchy, heterosexism, imperialism, American hegemony, and more. As a Mexican-American, when I say American hegemony is bad, I say it the way an anti-Zionist Jew says Zionism is bad. Acute systems target specific groups by race, religion, ethnicity, sex, gender identity, nationality, and more. But one system cuts across the most categories, including white supremacy, the biggest of the acute systems.

I am talking about capitalism, and even more so 21st-century, AI-powered, late-stage capitalism. This is what I thought “oligarchy” meant, but I have come to think the word is sometimes used to avoid saying capitalism. Capitalism cuts across all the others because it runs on the exponential drive for profit. A first-century letter in the New Testament put it this way: “For the love of money is the root of all kinds of evil” (1 Timothy 6:10).

Capitalism is a core root of oppression because it quite literally birthed white supremacy, through what W. E. B. Du Bois called the public and psychological wage of whiteness in Black Reconstruction in America (1935) and what David Roediger named The Wages of Whiteness (1991). So wokeai believes in worker cooperatives as “the first sprouts of the new” mode of production, in Marx’s phrase, an idea argued today by Bernard Harcourt, the Morning Star, and Carlos Garrido. It begins as a transitional living worker cooperative with a worker-owner count of one.
