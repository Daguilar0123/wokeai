# Social copy

Brand rules apply everywhere: **wokeai** lowercase, one word; the pronunciation line once per surface; no borrowed slogans; no sources named.

## Facebook

**Intro** (255-character field):

wokeai (pronounced wōk•ē•ī). A grassroots, open-source community for people using their own AI to push back against systemic oppression. Open tools, shared in common. Starting small. wokeainow.com

**About:**

wokeai is one word, always lowercase, and it's said *wōk•ē•ī*. The spelling says one thing to a glance. We'll tell you how it's said.

We're a grassroots community for people who use their own AI to push back against systemic oppression: students, independent researchers, organizers, and builders. Not a product. A place to gather, a list of open tools that already exist, and the people who want to build the next ones.

Three things hold it up. Open source: tools held in common, with the code where everyone can read it. Liberation: starting from the people with the least power, not the most. Community: nobody does this alone. The roots are the point.

We're in our first days and there's no budget, so we start with what we have. Read the tool list, bring the ones you use, and tell someone.

wokeainow.com · github.com/Daguilar0123/wokeai

## LinkedIn

**Overview** (2,000-character field; this is 1,891):

wokeai is a web app under construction. The community and the tool sharing are too. What’s here now is the idea, the brand, and the code (pun intended).

As a brand, wokeai is about using AI to fundamentally challenge systems of oppression, which come in many forms: white supremacy, patriarchy, heterosexism, imperialism, American hegemony, and more. Acute systems of oppression target specific groups by race, religion, ethnicity, sex, gender identity, nationality, and more. But one system cuts across the most categories, including white supremacy, the biggest of the acute systems.

I am talking about capitalism, and even more so 21st-century, AI-powered, late-stage capitalism. This is what I thought “oligarchy” meant, but I have come to wonder whether the word is deployed to avoid saying capitalism. Capitalism cuts across all the others because it runs on the exponential drive for profit. A first-century letter in the New Testament put it this way: “For the love of money is the root of all kinds of evil” (1 Timothy 6:10).

Capitalism is a core root of oppression because it quite literally birthed white supremacy, through what W. E. B. Du Bois called the public and psychological wage of whiteness in Black Reconstruction in America (1935) and what David Roediger named The Wages of Whiteness (1991). So wokeai believes in worker cooperatives as “the first sprouts of the new” mode of production, in Marx’s phrase, an idea argued today by Bernard Harcourt, the Morning Star, and Carlos Garrido. We begin as a transitional living worker cooperative with a worker-owner count of one.

Join us. The community space and the tool sharing are not built yet. Right now there is a repository, a list of tools, and each other. Read the covenant. Bring the tools you use. Tell someone who is being crushed right now that there is a place. Join on GitHub: github.com/Daguilar0123/wokeai

On July 23, 2025, the White House issued Executive Order 14319, “Preventing Woke AI in the Federal Government.” It tells federal agencies to buy only large language models built to what it calls Unbiased AI Principles, and it spells out the bias to be kept out. In the order’s own words, DEI in AI includes “concepts like critical race theory, transgenderism, unconscious bias, intersectionality, and systemic racism.”

Read that list again. By order, naming systemic racism is the bias. The AI the government buys will be AI that has been built not to say the word.

wokeai runs the other way: people using AI they own, on their own machines, to name those systems and work against them.

That is the “woke” in wokeai, and we say it out loud.
