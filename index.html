wokeai

One word. Say it whoa‑keyah.

What this is

A grassroots community for people using their own AI to push back against systemic oppression, and building the tools to do it in the open.

Students, independent researchers, organizers, and builders. People who were told that paying attention is an insult, and who kept paying attention anyway. The name reads one way on the page and sounds like something else out loud. That gap is the point: it rewards a closer look, and it takes back a word that was turned into a slur.

We are starting with what we have: a place to gather, a list of tools that already exist, and the people who want to build the next ones. The work is underneath, and it is shared.

Open tools you can use today

A starter list of existing open-source projects for running and studying AI on your own terms. We don't make these; we point to them. More to come as the community adds what it uses.

  • OllamaRun open language models on your own computer.
  • llama.cppThe engine many local-model tools are built on. Runs on modest hardware.
  • Open WebUIA self-hosted chat interface for local models.
  • Hugging FaceWhere open models and datasets are published and shared.
  • JupyterNotebooks for research you can show your work in.
