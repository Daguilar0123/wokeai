# wokeai

One lowercase word. Pronounced *wōk•ē•ī*.

Brand: **wokeai**. Domains: **wokeainow.com** and **wokeainow.app**, both registered at GoDaddy on October 18, 2026 (Danny's purchase; the .com was $45.99 for three years, then $22.99/yr). The .com is the website; the .app is held for the app that comes later and should redirect to the .com until then.

An aspirational brand and community: a website and social profiles gathering people around grassroots resistance to systemic oppression, from students and independent researchers using their own AI to tools built and shared in the open. Pillars: open source, liberation, community. It answers Executive Order 14319, "Preventing Woke AI in the Federal Government" (July 23, 2025), and is informed by the Black liberation struggle in America.

Not an AI product yet. As of October 2026 there is no budget, so this starts as a site that spreads awareness, shares existing tools, and gathers people who want to build.

## Decisions, dated

Danny decided (October 18, 2026):

- **Mark A is the symbol.** Chosen on sight from the first preview, with one tweak: the roots must read as a git graph with a main branch, two open branches, and one merged branch. Three revisions followed to stop the merge bulge and a lower branch from forming a letter R.
- **Mark B is not scrapped.** It is held for the podcast.
- **Theology stays implicit** in the public brand. The images carry it; the site speaks in liberation and open-source language and is interfaith by default. Sources get named on Tattered Rose, not here. **Superseded later the same day (October 18, 2026):** after reading the stock Contributor Covenant and finding it had no perspective on power, Danny decided the site speaks in his full leftist-political-theology voice, the covenant page especially and the homepage too. The register stays secular (he says "a brown Palestinian Jew named Jesus," "by the grace of the universe"; panentheist, not pantheist; no supernatural claims), and the moral rhetoric is strong on purpose. The brand book's "Voice" line ("no quotation of historical figures, nothing that implies violence") still holds; the covenant paraphrases a text, names no one's endorsement, and rejects armed conflict in so many words.
- **The covenant, and the homepage, carry the vision** (Danny, October 18, 2026): the love of money and the love of power are the root; "cruel" and "inhumane" are earned with reporting that can be checked (children in court alone, citizen children deported, the Fort Bliss camp); Democrats held to the same standard as Republicans; every leftist movement that won took up arms, but no serious person should propose armed conflict against the United States, because a better way exists and it is not a "third way"; the way is to go directly at who owns the means of production through **transitional living worker co-ops**: worker-owned, pay ratio at most 3:1, profits split close to evenly, decision-making hierarchical at first because one person is building it and says so, a program that starts at the roots of people's trauma and matches them to work they are passionate about and skilled at, and real equity on the way out.
- **The old placeholder (an open eye with rays) is replaced.** It read as the watcher, and the whole point is the watched being heard.
- **The spoken form is *wohk/ē/eye*.** An earlier "whoa-keyah" came from misreading *wokeai* as *wokeia*; Danny caught it the same day. The pronunciation line sits under the wordmark on purpose: the spelling says one thing to a glance, and the brand tells you how it is actually said. **Respelled *wōk•ē•ī* on October 21, 2026 (by way of *wōk/ē/ī* earlier that day), same sound; see the decisions below.**
- **Domains: wokeainow.com and wokeainow.app.** The exact-name .com is a $3,762 premium and woke.ai is taken; wokeainow keeps the brand spelled exactly and only adds "now." Both bought October 18, 2026. The brand stays "wokeai," never "wokeainow." No domain was bought for the podcast name.
- **License: AGPL-3.0 for the code; the name and marks reserved; the research writing CC BY-SA 4.0.** Reached by questioning, not by default, October 18, 2026. Danny's first instinct was that it depends who takes the tool (a co-op versus a surveillance vendor); told that no open-source license can tell them apart, he chose the rule that binds everyone equally to share back. Asked *when* an improver owes the public their changes, he chose "as soon as people are using it, any way," hosted or installed, which is the clause that makes it AGPL rather than GPL. The marks stay out of the code license so that using the code is not permission to wear the flag; the community-use policy for the name and marks is [`BRAND.md`](BRAND.md), written the same day. The research memo is prose, so it carries the share-alike license for text.
- **The podcast is *wahkeeye*, tagline "pronounced wōke•ĀĪ."** Danny proposed flipping the whole brand to this spelling (October 18, 2026) and decided against it the same day: the main brand stays one legible lowercase word, and the spelled-from-sound name goes to the one medium that is heard rather than seen. The letters are Google's respelling of *wok·e·ai* (wok as in the pan: *wäk*); the "woke" lives in the tagline, which is the joke. Mark B is its mark. No domain was bought for it (Danny, October 18, 2026: the domains are wokeainow.com and wokeainow.app only); the show will live under the main site. Known risks, recorded so they get tested before launch: the word reads like a Pacific Northwest Indigenous place name (cf. Wahkiakum, Wahkon); it is hard to spell from hearing; it puts "eye" back in a name.

## Brand

Design system **version 2**, October 18, 2026 (supersedes version 1 of the same day). The full brand book is [`design/system/README.md`](design/system/README.md); this is its short form.

**Intro.** wokeai (pronounced wōk•ē•ī). A grassroots, open-source community for people using their own AI to push back against systemic oppression. Open tools, shared in common. Starting small. wokeainow.com

**The name.** *wokeai*: one word, always lowercase, even at the start of a sentence. Never *Wokeai*, never *woke AI* when you mean the brand, never *wokeainow* (that is only the domain). The pronunciation line, `pronounced wōk•ē•ī`, sits under the wordmark wherever there is room, once per surface. The secondary wordmark is `wōke•ĀĪ`.

**The mark.** Three clumps of grass above a ground line; below, each clump has its own roots drawn as a network. Grass is `accent`, the ground line `earth`, roots and nodes `root`, the traveling light `light-core` with a `light-halo` glow; those last three tokens are for the mark only, never text. On an `accent` background use the blueprint in `surface`, never the color mark. Minimum 24px beside text, 32px alone; below 32px use the favicon cut. Clear space one eighth of the ground line on every side. No shadows, no outlines, no recoloring outside the tokens. The brand never shows an eye, rays, fist, chains, torch, lightbulb, face or likeness, or an X-shaped mark. The SVGs are final: use them as given, don't redraw them.

**Voice.** Plain, direct, warm. Short sentences. No borrowed slogans, no quotation of historical figures, nothing that implies violence or claims anyone's endorsement.

**Podcast.** *wahkeeye*, with the Heard mark. Its tagline is not decided ("spelled wokeai" or "spelled wōke•ĀĪ"); the lockup stays as it was until it is.

- **The home page speaks in Danny's words (October 19, 2026).** The October 18 rewrite's voice wasn't his. He supplied the copy: capitalism, and 21st-century AI-powered late-stage capitalism above all, as the system that cuts across the acute systems of oppression; capitalism as the birth of white supremacy via the wages of whiteness (Du Bois 1935, Roediger 1991); worker cooperatives as Marx's "first sprouts of the new" (Capital III, ch. 27); the app as branches per system of oppression; wokeai as a transitional living worker cooperative with a worker-owner count of one. Decided the same day: the money line is cited to 1 Timothy 6:10; the anti-Zionist comparison stays on the landing page; the full text is on the home page; an under-construction notice sits under the header and in Join.
- **The pronunciation line is respelled *wōk/ē/ī* (Danny, October 21, 2026).** Same three syllables as *wohk/ē/eye* (woke, ee, eye), now in dictionary macrons. Danny's reason: "it almost looks like DEI," the word Executive Order 14319 is aimed at. He first proposed an A/B test, then in the same message said to replace all instances. Replaced wherever the line is current: the home page (header, "The name," meta and share tags), the covenant header, `preview.html`, `BRAND.md`, this README's intro and name rules, the brand book's name rules and the `tokens.json` caption sample, the Lockup and Cover component previews, `design/social-copy.md`, and the paste sheet in `docs/plans/social-handles.md` (bio counts now 141, 150, and 73). Dated records keep the old spelling with a note: the October 18 spoken-form decision above, the brand book's superseded site-copy block, and the October 18 Claude Design prompt and brief. Images with the line drawn in still say *wohk/ē/eye* until they are re-rendered (see Not done yet). The secondary wordmark *wōke•ĀĪ* is unchanged. **Superseded later the same day: the slashes became interpuncts (next entry).**
- **The separator is the interpunct: *wōk•ē•ī* (Danny, October 21, 2026, later the same day).** In his words: "I like the • instead of the / from now on. Change everywhere, including the images." The • is the same character as in the secondary wordmark *wōke•ĀĪ* (U+2022), so the two now read as a deliberate pair. Danny set the contrast himself in the tagline of the new LinkedIn page: "It's pronounced wōk•ē•ī not Wōke•ĀĪ" (quoted as he typed it; elsewhere the brand keeps *wōke•ĀĪ* lowercase). That settles the look-alike question raised earlier the same day: the home page's "The name" block makes the same contrast on purpose. Every current text instance now reads *wōk•ē•ī*; bio lengths are unchanged (141, 150, 73). The images were handed to the wokeai-brand-symbol session to re-render and to pass on to the sessions that own them.

## Where the ideas come from

- *Grass roots*: Malcolm X, "Message to the Grass Roots," King Solomon Baptist Church, Detroit, November 10, 1963, is the one speech whose transcript excerpt was verified during research. The brand borrows the idea of the field, not any quotation, likeness, or signature.
- *Roots as a commons*: open-source forking, and the symbiosis image in Danny's own writing (2014, 2025): what sustains a thing is the network underneath it, shared.
- *The heard cry*: the inversion of the surveillance eye. Being awake, in the liberation tradition, is hearing the cry, not watching from above.
